# My Project brief 

## The Question 
Which areas of Ago-Iwoye have the highest potential for groundwater occurrence?

## The data I need

- Study area boundary – GADM / GRID3 – GeoPackage/Vector – ~2.6 MB for GADM
- Geology – NGSA – PDF/GIS layer – Size depends on map
- Elevation – Copernicus DEM GLO-30 – GeoTIFF – 30 m – Tile dependent
- Slope – Derived from DEM in QGIS – GeoTIFF – Generated locally
- Drainage/flow accumulation – HydroSHEDS – GeoTIFF – ~90 m – Tile dependent
- Rivers and streams – HydroRIVERS – Shapefile – ~108 MB for Africa
- Lineaments – Derived from DEM/hillshade – Vector – Generated locally
- Soil – SoilGrids – GeoTIFF – 250 m – Area dependent
- Land cover – ESA WorldCover – GeoTIFF – 10 m – Tile dependent
- Rainfall – CHIRPS – GeoTIFF – 0.05° (~5 km) – Depends on period
- Settlement extents – GRID3 – GeoPackage/Vector – Size depends on download
- Population – GRID3 – Raster – 100 m – Size depends on download
- Roads – GRID3 – Vector – Size depends on download
